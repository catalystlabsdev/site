FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is node operator as a service?

We run blockchain validators and nodes for institutions, end to end. You hold the stake and the mandate. We run the infrastructure.

Which networks do you support?

Ethereum and Canton Network validators, plus Bitcoin, Solana, Aptos and NEAR nodes. New networks on request.

Where does your infrastructure run?

Across AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, CoreWeave and Oracle Cloud in North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific, in separate accounts and regions.

How do you handle keys?

Generated offline, stored in remote signers with slashing protection, never on the host that talks to the network. Access is hardware-backed and least-privilege.

How do we work with you?

Email us. We scope the validators, regions and reporting you need and get them running.
